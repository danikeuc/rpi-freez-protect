# RPi Freeze Protect v1.2.0

Weather assistance, forecast display data and reliable paired DRAIN recovery.
This release publishes the Pi component. Dial firmware and Roon bridge are
separate components; this publication does not update or retag them.

## Changes

- Opt-in `weather_assisted` mode: today and the next four local forecast days
  must each have a finite minimum of at least 5 C before AUTO is eligible.
- Cold, missing, invalid, stale or failed forecasts return to manual fallback.
  Deliberate manual showers remain bounded to 1-10 minutes; weather changes and
  duplicate commands do not extend an accepted interval.
- Durable USER_OFF and interruption/fault inhibition prevent an unexpected
  reopening. STOP survives restart; a fresh deliberate START is required to
  clear USER_OFF. Enabling a preference alone does not clear STOP.
- Scoped, revision-checked weather preferences and explicit START/STOP APIs,
  with bounded forecast acquisition separated from the control loop.
- Five-day dates, minima and optional WMO weather codes for the companion UI.
  Weather codes and PT100/MAX31865 telemetry are informational.
- **P1 fix:** an unsuccessful paired DRAIN stays pending. The independent GPIO
  loop retries until both outputs are confirmed high, including expiry, explicit
  DRAIN and emergency DRAIN following an unsuccessful SUPPLY. Unresolved DRAIN
  rejects SUPPLY; an old RENEW cannot reopen after recovery.

The paired protocol remains version 2 and the independent running-daemon lease
remains 60 seconds. DRAIN is logical 0 but GPIO high/high for this installation;
SUPPLY is logical 1 but GPIO low/low.

## Verification and limits

Fresh checks and their exact source revision are published in `VERIFICATION.md`
and `manifest.json`. Offline coverage includes failed writes/readbacks, repeated
DRAIN failure, recovery without Hub traffic, stale RENEW rejection, persistence,
weather eligibility and API/daemon integration.

No live GPIO fault injection or physical valve movement/failure test is claimed.
Continuous-duty suitability, power-domain failures, daemon crash/hang and actual
valve position remain physical acceptance gaps. A running retry loop cannot
repair permanent hardware failure. PT100 remains informational in this mode.
Known companion admin idle-session and PIN-only physical recovery gaps are
outside this Pi release and are not represented as fixed.

## Installation and recovery

Use the complete source/deployment archive and the documented
[commissioning procedure](../../deployment/WORKSTATION_CODEX_COMMISSIONING.md)
and [weather runbook](../operations/weather-assisted-shower.md). The wheel alone
**does not install the paired GPIO daemon or Node-RED flow**. Preserve protected
credentials, the complete compatible stack, SQLite database and matching
weather identity. Keep valve power disconnected during separately authorized
maintenance and require startup DRAIN and paired high readback.

When extracting source or operator packages as root, use
`tar --no-same-owner --no-same-permissions -xzf <archive>` inside a fresh private
root-owned directory with `umask 077`. Check SHA256SUMS first. Do not rerun the
one-off P1 installer on an already updated daemon.

Retain the pre-update backup. A rollback must keep the Hub stopped/drained and
restore a coherent compatible stack; do not downgrade a live active interval.
The predecessor daemon contains P1, so restoration is recovery work, not a
claim that the defect is fixed. No automatic rollback is part of publication.

Existing v1.0.0 and v1.1.0 tags and assets remain unchanged. Publishing v1.2.0
neither enables AUTO nor authorizes START/SUPPLY or connecting valve power.
